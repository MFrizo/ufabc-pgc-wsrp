# M6 — Fleet Size and Mix Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (FSMVRPTW)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m6` in `src/models/model_6.py`
- **Case:** 7
- **Instance settings:** `num_brokers = 3` (more than needed), `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = RUSH_HOURS`

[M5](m5.md) where the number of brokers who leave the agency is minimized first and their total time out of the agency second. The model is built by `build_model_m5` and only the objective is replaced, so the sets, variables and constraints are exactly M5's.

## Sets

Same as [M5](m5.md): $V$, $C$, $K$ and $\mathcal{P}$.

## Parameters

All of [M5](m5.md)'s parameters, plus the weights of the objective:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $W_2$ | `model.time_weight` | `time_weight` (optional, default $1$) | Weight of the time out of the agency |
| $W_1$ | `model.fleet_weight` | `fleet_weight` (optional) | Weight of each broker who leaves the agency |

Unless given, $W_1$ is computed by `compute_fleet_weight`, large enough that one broker fewer always outweighs any saving in time:

$$
W_1 = W_2 \cdot |K| \cdot L + 1, \qquad
L = \max_{i \in V} (l_i + s_i) + \max_{p \in \mathcal{P}}\ \max_{i, j \in V} t^p_{ij} - e_0
$$

$L$ bounds the time any broker can be out: no one leaves before $e_0$ or gets back later than the latest departure plus the slowest trip.

## Decision variables

Same as [M5](m5.md): $x_{ijk}$, $w_i$, $w^{dep}_k$, $w^{ret}_k$, $z_{ip}$ and $z^0_{kp}$. There is no separate variable for a broker working; the brokers used are counted by their departures from the agency.

## Objective function

Minimize the brokers who leave the agency first and their total time out of it second (`model.obj`):

$$
\min\ W_1 \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j \in C} x_{0jk} + W_2 \sum_{k \in K} \big(w^{ret}_k - w^{dep}_k\big)
$$

## Constraints

Constraints (1) to (8) of [M5](m5.md), unchanged.

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & W_1 \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j \in C} x_{0jk} + W_2 \sum_{k \in K} \big(w^{ret}_k - w^{dep}_k\big) \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \text{constraints (1)–(8) of M5}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- The weights make the objective lexicographic: the fewest brokers first, then the least time out of the agency among the plans with that many brokers.
- The name follows the literature, where the "mix" is a choice between vehicle types with different capacities and costs. Here every broker is identical and has no capacity, so the model is a VRPTW with fleet minimization.
