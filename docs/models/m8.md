# M8 — Human-Centric Dynamic Open Multi-Depot VRP with Soft Time Windows and Site-Dependency (HC-DOMDVRPTW-SD)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m8` in `src/models/model_8.py`
- **Case:** 8.b
- **Instance settings:** `num_brokers = 3`, `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = NORMAL_SPEED`, `days_off = 1`, `split_shifts = True`. Every visit is booked: `--fixed-ratio` is ignored (`STRICT_TIME_MODELS`)

The brokers' point of view. Each broker who works has his own morning shift, from his home to the place he picks for lunch, and his own afternoon shift, from that place back home. No visit may end after the end of its shift, and brokers on their day off do not work. Visits may start up to 10 minutes early or late, and each minute late is penalized. A weighted sum of distance and delay is minimized. The distance includes the trip from the last visit of each shift to its destination.

As in [M7](m7.md), the model optimizes the start of the day; re-running it when the visits change is left to the caller.

## Sets

Each shift of a broker who works is its own route $r$. With $R$ the set of shifts, broker $\kappa(r)$ works shift $r$:

- the morning shift (label `A`) goes from his home to his lunch spot;
- the afternoon shift (label `B`) goes from his lunch spot back home.

Shifts are named by broker and label, e.g. `1A` and `1B`. A broker on his day off has no shifts.

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $K = \{1, \dots, m\}$ | `model.K` | Brokers |
| $R = \{1, \dots, \lvert R \rvert\}$ | `model.R` | Shifts, two per broker who works |
| $C = \{1, \dots, n\}$ | `model.C` | Properties to visit |
| $D = \{d_r : r \in R\}$ | `model.D` | Origin of each shift, $d_r = n + r$ |
| $F = \{f_r : r \in R\}$ | `model.F` | Virtual end of each shift, $f_r = n + \lvert R \rvert + r$ |
| $V = C \cup D \cup F$ | `model.V` | All nodes |
| $\mathcal{A}$ | `model.ARCS` | Arcs $(i, j, r)$ shift $r$ may take |

Node $0$ (the agency) is not used. A shift goes from its origin to a property, from a property to another one, and from a property to the end of the shift:

$$
\mathcal{A} = \bigcup_{r \in R} \Big(
\{(d_r, j, r) : j \in C\}
\cup \{(i, j, r) : i \in C,\ j \in C \cup \{f_r\},\ j \ne i\}
\Big)
$$

## Parameters

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $\kappa(r)$ | `model.route_broker` | `shifts` | Broker of shift $r$ |
| — | `model.route_name` | — | Name of shift $r$, e.g. `1A` |
| $c^r_{ij}$ | `model.distance` | `distance_matrix`, `home_distances`, `lunch_spot_distances` | Distance of arc $(i, j, r)$ |
| $t^r_{ij}$ | `model.travel_time` | `travel_times[0]`, `home_travel_times[0]`, `lunch_spot_travel_times[0]` | Travel time of arc $(i, j, r)$ |
| $s_i$ | `model.service` | `service_times` | Duration at node $i$; $0$ at $d_r$ and $f_r$ |
| $h_i$ | `model.scheduled` | `earliest_start` | Scheduled start of the visit at property $i$ |
| $\tau$ | `model.tolerance` | — (`DELAY_TOLERANCE` = 10) | Most minutes a visit may start early or late |
| $E_r$, $L_r$ | `model.shift_start`, `model.shift_end` | `shifts` | Start and hard end of shift $r$ |
| $P_{ir}$ | `model.eligibility` | `assigned_broker` | $1$ if the broker of shift $r$ may visit property $i$ |
| $H$ | bounds of `model.w` | — | Latest start time of any node |
| $M$ | `model.big_m` | `big_m` (optional) | Big-M constant |
| $W_{dist}$ | `model.distance_weight` | `distance_weight` (optional, default 1) | Weight of the distance |
| $W_{atraso}$ | `model.delay_weight` | `delay_weight` (optional, default 10) | Weight of each minute of delay |

With $\text{orig}_r$ and $\text{dest}_r$ the origin and destination places of shift $r$ (the home or the lunch spot of broker $\kappa(r)$), the trip to the end of a shift is the trip to its destination:

$$
c^r_{ij} =
\begin{cases}
c_{ij} & i, j \in C \\
c^{\text{orig}_r}_{j} & i = d_r,\ j \in C \\
c^{\text{dest}_r}_{i} & i \in C,\ j = f_r
\end{cases}
$$

$t^r_{ij}$ follows the same cases with the travel times of the first (and only) period.

$P_{ir} = 1$ if $a_i \in \{0, \kappa(r)\}$, where $a_i$ is the broker already assigned to property $i$ ($0$ if none).

The generator draws each broker's shifts from `MORNING_SHIFT` and `AFTERNOON_SHIFT`: the morning starts between 08:00 and 09:00 and ends between 11:30 and 12:30; the afternoon starts between 13:00 and 14:00 and ends between 17:00 and 18:00. The gap between the two is the lunch break.

The bounds computed from the data are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= \max\Big(\max_{r \in R} L_r,\ \max_{i \in C} (h_i + \tau + s_i)\Big) + \max_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} t^r_{ij} \\
M &= H + \max_{i \in V} s_i + \max_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} t^r_{ij}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Decision variables

| Symbol | Pyomo | Domain | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x_{ijr}$ | `model.x` | $\{0, 1\}$, $(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}$ | $1$ if, in shift $r$, the broker travels directly from $i$ to $j$ |
| $w_i$ | `model.w` | $[0, H]$, $i \in V$ | Start time at node $i$: of the visit, of leaving the origin ($d_r$) or of arriving at the destination ($f_r$) |
| $\delta_i$ | `model.delay` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $i \in C$ | Minutes late of the visit at $i$ (*atraso*) |

## Objective function

Minimize the weighted distance and delay (`model.obj`):

$$
\min\ W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} c^r_{ij}\, x_{ijr} + W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C} \delta_i
$$

## Constraints

Sums over $j$ (or $i$) below run over the arcs of $\mathcal{A}$ only.

**(1) Single service** — every property is left exactly once, in exactly one shift (`single_service_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{r \in R} \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} = 1 \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(2) Compatibility** (`compatibility_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} \le P_{ir} \qquad \forall i \in C,\ r \in R
$$

**(3) Origin departure** — each shift leaves its origin at most once (`origin_departure_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r} \le 1 \qquad \forall r \in R
$$

**(4) End of the shift** — a shift that leaves its origin ends at its destination (`end_arrival_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i \in C} x_{i f_r r} = \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r} \qquad \forall r \in R
$$

**(5) Flow conservation** (`flow_conservation_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i : (i, h, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihr} - \sum_{j : (h, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjr} = 0 \qquad \forall h \in C,\ r \in R
$$

**(6) Time flow** (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} - M\,(1 - x_{ijr}) \le w_j \qquad \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}
$$

**(7) Soft time windows** (`early_start_constraint`, `soft_window_constraint`, `delay_tolerance_constraint`):

$$
w_i \ge h_i - \tau, \qquad w_i \le h_i + \delta_i, \qquad \delta_i \le \tau \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(8) Hard shift limits** — a shift doesn't leave its origin before it starts, and its last visit ends by the end of the shift (`shift_start_constraint`, `shift_end_constraint`):

$$
w_{d_r} \ge E_r \quad \forall r \in R, \qquad
w_i + s_i - M\,(1 - x_{i f_r r}) \le L_r \quad \forall i \in C,\ r \in R
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} c^r_{ij}\, x_{ijr} + W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C} \delta_i \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{r \in R} \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} \le P_{ir} && \forall i \in C,\ r \in R \\
& \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r} \le 1 && \forall r \in R \\
& \sum_{i \in C} x_{i f_r r} = \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r} && \forall r \in R \\
& \sum_{i : (i, h, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihr} - \sum_{j : (h, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjr} = 0 && \forall h \in C,\ r \in R \\
& w_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} - M\,(1 - x_{ijr}) \le w_j && \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& h_i - \tau \le w_i \le h_i + \delta_i,\quad \delta_i \le \tau && \forall i \in C \\
& w_{d_r} \ge E_r && \forall r \in R \\
& w_i + s_i - M\,(1 - x_{i f_r r}) \le L_r && \forall i \in C,\ r \in R \\
& x_{ijr} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& 0 \le w_i \le H && \forall i \in V \\
& \delta_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in C
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- **Brokers enter only through their shifts.** $K$ is declared but no constraint is indexed by it. A broker on his day off has no shifts, so he can't do any visit.
- **Hard shift end:** (8) applies to the end of the last visit. The trip to the destination counts in the distance and in $w_{f_r}$, but it may end after $L_r$.
- **Lunch** is the gap between the two shifts and has no duration of its own. Nothing links the morning shift's arrival at the lunch spot ($w_{f_r}$ of shift `A`) to the afternoon shift's departure from it ($w_{d_r}$ of shift `B`), which is bound only by $E_r$.
- **No fleet minimization, overtime or day-length limit:** the shifts are the only limit on each broker's day.
- **Windows** read only the scheduled start $h_i$, as in [M7](m7.md).
- **Units:** the objective adds distance units to minutes of delay, as in [M7](m7.md).
