# M2.a — M2 with Tightened and Lifted MTZ Time-Flow Constraints (HC-DOMDVRPTW-SD)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m2_a` in `src/models/model_2_a.py`
- **Role:** tightened and lifted reformulation of [M2](m2.md) without a single global Big-M constant
- **Instance settings:** same as [M2](m2.md) (`num_brokers = 3`, `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = NORMAL_SPEED`, `days_off = 1`, `split_shifts = True`; default seed 8). `--fixed-ratio` in `main.py` overrides the share of booked visits from `0.0` to `1.0`.

[M2](m2.md) deactivates the time-flow inequality $w_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ijr}) \le w_j$, the hard shift-end inequality $w_i + s_i - M(1 - x_{i f_r r}) \le L_r$, and the shift-span inequality $\sigma_r \ge w_{f_r} - w_{d_r} - M(1 - \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r})$ with a single global constant $M = H + \max s_i + \max t^r_{ij}$. When $x_{ijr}$ is fractional in the linear relaxation, that global $M$ leaves the time and shift-span variables loosely coupled to the routing variables.

M2.a keeps the exact same feasible set and objective function as [M2](m2.md) for every `fixed_ratio`, and replaces all global Big-M constants with four tightenings:

1. **Time-window preprocessing (domain reduction)** across shifts $r \in R$ by iterative forward and backward network reachability, plus fixing time-infeasible arcs ($a_i + s_i > L_r$ or $a_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} > b_j$) to zero;
2. **Shift-specific tightened $M^r_{ij} = \max(0,\ b_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} - a_j)$**, aggregated across shifts on property-to-property pairs $(i, j) \in C \times C$;
3. **Desrochers and Laporte (1991) lifted time-window bounds**, bidirectional two-cycle elimination, and a **shift-lifted hard shift-end inequality**;
4. **Tightened shift-span constant $M^{\text{span}}_r = \max(0,\ b_{f_r} - a_{d_r})$** together with a Big-M-free linear travel-and-service lower bound on $\sigma_r$.

## Sets

All of [M2](m2.md)'s sets ($K$, $R$, $C$, $C_h$, $C_w$, $D$, $F$, $V$ and $\mathcal{A}$), plus the ordered and unordered property pairs:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $P_{\text{ord}} = \lbrace (i, j) \in C \times C : i \ne j \rbrace$ | `model.PAIRS` | Ordered pairs of distinct properties |
| $P_{<} = \lbrace (i, j) \in C \times C : i < j \rbrace$ | `model.UPPER_PAIRS` | Unordered pairs of distinct properties |

## Parameters and time-window preprocessing

All of [M2](m2.md)'s parameters, except that the global constant `model.big_m` is replaced by tightened node windows $[a_i, b_i]$, arc-specific coefficients $M^r_{ij}$, and shift-specific span coefficients $M^{\text{span}}_r$:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $a_i, b_i$ | `model.window_start`, `model.window_end` | Tightened earliest and latest start time at node $i \in V$ |
| $M^r_{ij}$ | `model.arc_big_m` | Tightened time-flow coefficient of arc $(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}$ |
| $M^{\text{span}}_r$ | `model.span_big_m` | Tightened shift-span coefficient of shift $r \in R$ |

### 1. Pre-processing time windows (domain reduction)

Each node $i \in V$ starts with its initial time window $[a_i^{(0)}, b_i^{(0)}]$:

$$
[a_i^{(0)}, b_i^{(0)}] =
\begin{cases}
[\max(0,\ h_i - \tau),\ h_i + \tau] & i \in C_h \\
[e_i,\ l_i] & i \in C_w \\
[E_r,\ \max(E_r,\ L_r)] & i = d_r \in D \\
[E_r,\ H] & i = f_r \in F
\end{cases}
$$

Let $\mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}$ be the set of compatible, unblocked arcs $(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}$ that satisfy $a_i + s_i \le L_r$ and $a_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} \le b_j$. Before building the constraints, $a_j$ and $b_i$ are tightened iteratively until convergence for all $i, j \in C$, taking into account the hard shift end $L_r$:

$$
a_j \leftarrow \max\Big(a_j,\ \min_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}} (a_i + s_i + t^r_{ij})\Big), \qquad
b_i \leftarrow \min\Big(b_i,\ \max_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}} \min(L_r - s_i,\ b_j - s_i - t^r_{ij})\Big)
$$

The shift origin and destination upper bounds are tightened from the eligible properties $i \in C$ of shift $r$:

$$
b_{d_r} = \max\Big(a_{d_r},\ \min\Big(L_r,\ \max_{(d_r, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}} (b_j - t^r_{d_r j})\Big)\Big), \qquad
b_{f_r} = \max\Big(a_{f_r},\ \max_{(i, f_r, r) \in \mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}} \big(\min(b_i,\ L_r - s_i) + s_i + t^r_{i f_r}\big)\Big)
$$

Any arc $(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}$ with $i \in C$ and $a_i + s_i > L_r$, or with $a_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} > b_j$, cannot be used in any feasible schedule and is fixed to zero:

$$
x_{ijr} = 0 \qquad \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \text{ with } (i \in C \text{ and } a_i + s_i > L_r) \text{ or } a_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} > b_j
$$

### 2. Tightening $M^r_{ij}$ and $M^{\text{span}}_r$

For each arc $(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}$ and each shift $r \in R$:

$$
M^r_{ij} = \max\Big(0,\ b_i + s_i + t^r_{ij} - a_j\Big) \qquad \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}, \qquad
M^{\text{span}}_r = \max\Big(0,\ b_{f_r} - a_{d_r}\Big) \qquad \forall r \in R
$$

## Decision variables and objective function

Same as [M2](m2.md): $x_{ijr} \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$, $w_i \in [a_i, b_i]$, $\delta_i \ge 0$ (with $\delta_i = 0$ for $i \in C_w$), $\sigma_r \ge 0$, $\omega_k \ge 0$ and $\Omega \ge 0$, with the same objective function (`model.obj`).

## Constraints

Constraints (1) to (5), (7), (10) and (11) of [M2](m2.md) are unchanged. Constraints (6), (8) and (9) are replaced by the tightened and lifted inequalities below.

**(6a) Shift-lifted time flow between properties** (`pair_time_flow_constraint`). Because at most one shift traverses $(i, j) \in P_{\text{ord}}$, summing $M^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr}$ over $r \in R$ into a single row replaces $|R|$ separate rows with one tighter inequality:

$$
w_i - w_j + \sum_{r \in R} M^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} \le b_i - a_j \qquad \forall (i, j) \in P_{\text{ord}}
$$

**(6b) Arc-specific time flow on origin and destination arcs** (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i - w_j + M^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} \le b_i - a_j \qquad \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \text{ with } (i, j) \notin P_{\text{ord}}
$$

**(6c) Desrochers and Laporte (1991) lifted time-window bounds** (`lifted_early_constraint`, `lifted_late_constraint`):

$$
w_i \ge a_i + \sum_{(j, i, r) \in \mathcal{A}} \max\Big(0,\ a_j + s_j + t^r_{ji} - a_i\Big) x_{jir} \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

$$
w_i \le b_i - \sum_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} \max\Big(0,\ b_i - \min(L_r - s_i,\ b_j - s_i - t^r_{ij})\Big) x_{ijr} \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(6d) Bidirectional two-cycle elimination** (`two_cycle_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{r \in R} (x_{ijr} + x_{jir}) \le 1 \qquad \forall (i, j) \in P_{<}
$$

**(8) Lifted hard shift limits** (`shift_start_constraint`, `shift_end_constraint`, `shift_duration_constraint`, `fleet_duration_constraint`). Because every property $i \in C$ is visited in exactly one shift $r \in R$, and every visit in shift $r$ must finish by $L_r$, the $|C| \times |R|$ Big-M rows $w_i + s_i - M(1 - x_{i f_r r}) \le L_r$ are replaced by a single shift-aggregated upper bound per property:

$$
w_{d_r} \ge E_r \quad \forall r \in R, \qquad
w_i + \sum_{r \in R} \max\Big(0,\ b_i + s_i - L_r\Big) \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} \le b_i \quad \forall i \in C
$$

together with the shift and fleet duration bounds from [M2](m2.md).

**(9) Tightened shift span and linear duration lower bound** (`shift_span_constraint`, `shift_span_duration_constraint`):

$$
\sigma_r \ge w_{f_r} - w_{d_r} - M^{\text{span}}_r \Big(1 - \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r}\Big) \qquad \forall r \in R
$$

$$
\sigma_r \ge \sum_{(i, j) : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} (s_i + t^r_{ij})\ x_{ijr} \qquad \forall r \in R
$$

The second inequality requires no Big-M at all: when shift $r$ is unused, all $x_{ijr} = 0$ so the right-hand side is zero; when shift $r$ is used, the clock span $w_{f_r} - w_{d_r}$ is at least the sum of service and travel times along the shift, strengthening the linear relaxation of $\sigma_r$, $\omega_k$ and $\Omega$.

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \alpha \sum_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} c^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} + W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C_h} \delta_i + W_{fair}\ \Omega \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{r \in R} \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} \le P_{ir} && \forall i \in C,\ r \in R \\
& \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r} \le 1 && \forall r \in R \\
& \sum_{i \in C} x_{i f_r r} = \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r} && \forall r \in R \\
& \sum_{i : (i, h, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihr} - \sum_{j : (h, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjr} = 0 && \forall h \in C,\ r \in R \\
& w_i - w_j + \sum_{r \in R} M^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} \le b_i - a_j && \forall (i, j) \in P_{\text{ord}} \\
& w_i - w_j + M^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} \le b_i - a_j && \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A},\ (i, j) \notin P_{\text{ord}} \\
& \sum_{r \in R} (x_{ijr} + x_{jir}) \le 1 && \forall (i, j) \in P_{<} \\
& w_i \ge a_i + \sum_{(j, i, r) \in \mathcal{A}} \max(0,\ a_j + s_j + t^r_{ji} - a_i)\ x_{jir} && \forall i \in C \\
& w_i \le b_i - \sum_{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} \max(0,\ b_i - \min(L_r - s_i,\ b_j - s_i - t^r_{ij}))\ x_{ijr} && \forall i \in C \\
& h_i - \tau \le w_i \le h_i + \delta_i,\quad \delta_i \le \tau && \forall i \in C_h \\
& a_i \le w_i \le b_i,\quad \delta_i = 0 && \forall i \in C_w \\
& w_{d_r} \ge E_r && \forall r \in R \\
& w_i + \sum_{r \in R} \max(0,\ b_i + s_i - L_r) \sum_{j : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijr} \le b_i && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{(i, j) : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} s_i\ x_{ijr} + \sum_{\substack{(i, j) : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \\ j \in C}} t^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} \le L_r - E_r && \forall r \in R \\
& \sum_{i \in C} s_i + \sum_{\substack{(i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \\ j \in C}} t^r_{ij}\ x_{ijr} \le \sum_{r \in R} (L_r - E_r) \\
& \sigma_r \ge w_{f_r} - w_{d_r} - M^{\text{span}}_r \Big(1 - \sum_{j \in C} x_{d_r j r}\Big) && \forall r \in R \\
& \sigma_r \ge \sum_{(i, j) : (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A}} (s_i + t^r_{ij})\ x_{ijr} && \forall r \in R \\
& \omega_k = \sum_{r \in R_k} \sigma_r && \forall k \in K,\ R_k \ne \emptyset \\
& \omega_k = 0 && \forall k \in K,\ R_k = \emptyset \\
& \Omega \ge \omega_k && \forall k \in K,\ R_k \ne \emptyset \\
& x_{ijr} \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace && \forall (i, j, r) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& a_i \le w_i \le b_i && \forall i \in V \\
& \delta_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sigma_r \ge 0 && \forall r \in R \\
& \omega_k \ge 0 && \forall k \in K \\
& \Omega \ge 0
\end{aligned}
$$
