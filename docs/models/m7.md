# M7 — Multi-Objective Dynamic Open Multi-Depot VRP with Soft Time Windows and Site-Dependency (MO-DOMDVRPTW-SD)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m7` in `src/models/model_7.py`
- **Case:** 8.a
- **Instance settings:** `num_brokers = 3`, `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = NORMAL_SPEED`, `start_at_homes = True`, `lunch_break = (180, 360, 60)`, `max_day_length = 720`; default seed 8. Every visit is booked: `--fixed-ratio` is ignored (`STRICT_TIME_MODELS`); [M9](m9.md) is the variant with flexible visits

The agency's point of view. Every broker who works leaves his own home, takes a one-hour lunch break that starts between 11:00 and 14:00, and ends the day at his last visit, without returning. Visits may start up to 10 minutes early or late, and each minute late is penalized. A day may run past its regular end as penalized overtime, but never longer than 12 hours. The number of brokers is minimized first, then a weighted sum of distance, delay and overtime.

The model optimizes the start of the day. Re-running it from the brokers' current positions when the visits change is left to the caller, so the model itself is static.

## Sets

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $K = \{1, \dots, m\}$ | `model.K` | Brokers |
| $C = \{1, \dots, n\}$ | `model.C` | Properties to visit |
| $D = \{d_k : k \in K\}$ | `model.D` | Home of each broker, $d_k = n + k$ |
| $F = \{f_k : k \in K\}$ | `model.F` | Virtual end of each broker's day, $f_k = n + m + k$ |
| $A = \{a_k : k \in K\}$ | `model.A` | Virtual lunch of each broker, $a_k = n + 2m + k$ |
| $V = C \cup D \cup F \cup A$ | `model.V` | All nodes |
| $\mathcal{A}$ | `model.ARCS` | Arcs $(i, j, k)$ broker $k$ may take |

Node $0$ (the agency) is not used. The arcs of broker $k$ go from his home to a property or to lunch, from a property to another property, to lunch or to the end of the day, and from lunch to a property or to the end of the day:

$$
\mathcal{A} = \bigcup_{k \in K} \Big(
\{(d_k, j, k) : j \in C \cup \{a_k\}\}
\cup \{(i, j, k) : i \in C,\ j \in C \cup \{a_k, f_k\},\ j \ne i\}
\cup \{(a_k, j, k) : j \in C \cup \{f_k\}\}
\Big)
$$

## Parameters

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c^k_{ij}$ | `model.distance` | `distance_matrix`, `home_distances` | Distance of arc $(i, j, k)$ |
| $t^k_{ij}$ | `model.travel_time` | `travel_times[0]`, `home_travel_times[0]` | Travel time of arc $(i, j, k)$ |
| $s_i$ | `model.service` | `service_times` | Duration at node $i$ |
| $h_i$ | `model.scheduled` | `earliest_start` | Scheduled start of the visit at property $i$ |
| $\tau$ | `model.tolerance` | — (`DELAY_TOLERANCE` = 10) | Most minutes a visit may start early or late |
| $E_k$, $L_k$ | `model.shift_start`, `model.shift_end` | `shift_start`, `shift_end` | Start and regular end of broker $k$'s day (08:00 and 17:00) |
| $J_{max}$ | `model.max_day_length` | `max_day_length` (optional, default 720) | Longest day, from leaving home to the end of the last visit |
| $P_{ik}$ | `model.eligibility` | `assigned_broker` | $1$ if broker $k$ may visit property $i$ (see [M3](m3.md)) |
| $[\underline{\lambda}, \overline{\lambda}]$ | — (`LUNCH_WINDOW`) | — | Window for the start of lunch, $[180, 360]$ (11:00 to 14:00) |
| $H$ | bounds of `model.w` | — | Latest start time of any node |
| $M$ | `model.big_m` | `big_m` (optional) | Big-M constant |
| $W_{frota}$ | `model.fleet_weight` | `fleet_weight` (optional) | Weight of each broker who works |
| $W_{dist}$ | `model.distance_weight` | `distance_weight` (optional, default 1) | Weight of the distance |
| $W_{atraso}$ | `model.delay_weight` | `delay_weight` (optional, default 10) | Weight of each minute of delay |
| $W_{extra}$ | `model.overtime_weight` | `overtime_weight` (optional, default 100) | Weight of each minute of overtime |

The trips to and from the virtual nodes cost nothing:

$$
c^k_{ij} =
\begin{cases}
c_{ij} & i, j \in C \\
c^{home}_{kj} & i = d_k,\ j \in C \\
0 & \text{otherwise (arcs into or out of } a_k \text{, and into } f_k)
\end{cases}
$$

$t^k_{ij}$ follows the same cases with the travel times of the first (and only) period. The durations are $s_i$ for $i \in C$, $s_{a_k} = 60$ for lunch, and $0$ at $d_k$ and $f_k$.

The bounds and weights computed from the data are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= \max\Big(\max_{i \in C} (h_i + \tau + s_i),\ \overline{\lambda} + 60,\ \max_{k \in K} E_k\Big) \\
M &= H + \max_{i \in V} s_i + \max_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij} \\
W_{frota} &= (n + m) \max_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij}\, W_{dist} + n\, \tau\, W_{atraso} + \sum_{k \in K} \max(0,\ H - L_k)\, W_{extra} + 1
\end{aligned}
$$

$W_{frota}$ is larger than the distance, delay and overtime of any plan put together, so one broker fewer always comes first.

## Decision variables

| Symbol | Pyomo | Domain | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x_{ijk}$ | `model.x` | $\{0, 1\}$, $(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}$ | $1$ if broker $k$ travels directly from $i$ to $j$ |
| $y_k$ | `model.y` | $\{0, 1\}$, $k \in K$ | $1$ if broker $k$ works in the day |
| $w_i$ | `model.w` | $[0, H]$, $i \in V$ | Start time at node $i$: of the visit, of lunch, of leaving home ($d_k$) or of the end of the day ($f_k$) |
| $\delta_i$ | `model.delay` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $i \in C$ | Minutes late of the visit at $i$ (*atraso*) |
| $o_k$ | `model.overtime` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $k \in K$ | Minutes of overtime of broker $k$ (*horas extras*) |

## Objective function

Minimize the brokers who work first, then distance, delay and overtime (`model.obj`):

$$
\min\ W_{frota} \sum_{k \in K} y_k
+ W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk}
+ W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C} \delta_i
+ W_{extra} \sum_{k \in K} o_k
$$

## Constraints

Sums over $j$ (or $i$) below run over the arcs of $\mathcal{A}$ only.

**(1) Single service** — every property is left exactly once, by exactly one broker (`single_service_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} = 1 \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(2) Compatibility** (`compatibility_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} \qquad \forall i \in C,\ k \in K
$$

**(3) Home departure** — a broker who works leaves his home once (`home_departure_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j : (d_k, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{d_k j k} = y_k \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(4) End of the day** — a broker who works ends the day once (`end_arrival_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i : (i, f_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i f_k k} = y_k \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(5) Flow conservation** — a broker leaves every property, and his own lunch, that he enters (`flow_conservation_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i : (i, h, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j : (h, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjk} = 0 \qquad \forall k \in K,\ h \in C \cup \{a_k\}
$$

**(6) Time flow** (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - M\,(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j \qquad \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}
$$

**(7) Soft time windows** — a visit starts at most $\tau$ minutes before or after its scheduled time, and the minutes late are its delay (`early_start_constraint`, `soft_window_constraint`, `delay_tolerance_constraint`):

$$
w_i \ge h_i - \tau, \qquad w_i \le h_i + \delta_i, \qquad \delta_i \le \tau \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(8) Working day** — a broker doesn't leave home before his day starts, the end of his day past its regular end is overtime, and his whole day never exceeds the legal limit (`day_start_constraint`, `day_end_constraint`, `day_length_constraint`):

$$
w_{d_k} \ge E_k, \qquad w_{f_k} \le L_k + o_k, \qquad w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} \le J_{max} \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(9) Lunch break** — a broker who works goes through his lunch node, starting it inside the lunch window (`lunch_visit_constraint`, `lunch_window_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i : (i, a_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i a_k k} = y_k, \qquad \underline{\lambda} \le w_{a_k} \le \overline{\lambda} \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & W_{frota} \sum_{k \in K} y_k + W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk} + W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C} \delta_i + W_{extra} \sum_{k \in K} o_k \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} && \forall i \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{j : (d_k, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{d_k j k} = y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, f_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i f_k k} = y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, h, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j : (h, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjk} = 0 && \forall k \in K,\ h \in C \cup \{a_k\} \\
& w_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - M\,(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& h_i - \tau \le w_i \le h_i + \delta_i,\quad \delta_i \le \tau && \forall i \in C \\
& w_{d_k} \ge E_k,\quad w_{f_k} \le L_k + o_k,\quad w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} \le J_{max} && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, a_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i a_k k} = y_k,\quad \underline{\lambda} \le w_{a_k} \le \overline{\lambda} && \forall k \in K \\
& x_{ijk} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& y_k \in \{0, 1\},\ o_k \ge 0 && \forall k \in K \\
& 0 \le w_i \le H && \forall i \in V \\
& \delta_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in C
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- **Open routes:** the day ends at the end of the last visit (or of lunch, if it comes last). $f_k$ is virtual, so the trip back home is neither timed nor counted in the distance.
- **Lunch is a virtual node:** the arcs into and out of $a_k$ have zero distance and zero travel time. The trip from the last visit before lunch to the first one after it is therefore neither timed nor counted. If lunch is the first stop ($d_k \to a_k$), the trip from home to the first visit is free as well. The generator's hidden schedule does count the trip after lunch, from the last visit before it.
- **Windows:** only the scheduled start $h_i$ (`earliest_start`) is read; `latest_start` is not used. With `fixed_ratio = 1.0` the two are equal. A visit may start up to $\tau$ minutes early at no cost.
- **Overtime** is soft past $L_k$ (17:00) and hard at $J_{max}$ (12 hours from leaving home).
- A broker with $y_k = 0$ uses no arcs. His $w_{d_k}$, $w_{f_k}$ and $w_{a_k}$ still exist and still satisfy (8) and the lunch window, which is always possible.
- **Units:** $W_{dist}$ multiplies distance units, while $W_{atraso}$ and $W_{extra}$ multiply minutes. With one constant speed, distance and travel time are proportional apart from the rounding up to whole minutes.
