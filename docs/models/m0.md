# M0 — Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m0` in `src/models/model_0.py`
- **Case:** 1
- **Instance settings:** generator defaults (only the distances are read)

One broker leaves the agency, visits every property exactly once and returns to the agency, travelling the shortest total distance. Subtours are eliminated with the Miller–Tucker–Zemlin (MTZ) constraints.

## Sets

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $V = \{0, 1, \dots, n\}$ | `model.V` | All nodes; node $0$ is the agency |
| $C = V \setminus \{0\}$ | `model.C` | Properties to visit |

$n = |C|$ is the number of properties (`num_nodes - 1`).

## Parameters

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c_{ij}$ | `model.distance` | `distance_matrix` | Distance from node $i$ to node $j$ |

## Decision variables

| Symbol | Pyomo | Domain | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x_{ij}$ | `model.x` | $\{0, 1\}$, $i, j \in V$ | $1$ if the broker travels directly from $i$ to $j$ |
| $u_i$ | `model.u` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $i \in C$ | Position of property $i$ in the tour (MTZ order) |

## Objective function

Minimize the total distance travelled (`model.obj`):

$$
\min \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} c_{ij}\, x_{ij}
$$

## Constraints

**(1) Out-degree** — every node is left exactly once (`out_degree_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ij} = 1 \qquad \forall i \in V
$$

**(2) In-degree** — every node is entered exactly once (`in_degree_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne j} x_{ij} = 1 \qquad \forall j \in V
$$

**(3) Subtour elimination (MTZ)** — if the broker goes from $i$ to $j$, $j$ comes later in the tour (`mtz_constraint`):

$$
u_i - u_j + n\, x_{ij} \le n - 1 \qquad \forall i, j \in C,\ i \ne j
$$

**(4) No self-loops** (`no_self_loop_constraint`):

$$
x_{ii} = 0 \qquad \forall i \in V
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} c_{ij}\, x_{ij} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ij} = 1 && \forall i \in V \\
& \sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne j} x_{ij} = 1 && \forall j \in V \\
& u_i - u_j + n\, x_{ij} \le n - 1 && \forall i, j \in C,\ i \ne j \\
& x_{ii} = 0 && \forall i \in V \\
& x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall i, j \in V \\
& u_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in C
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- The order variables $u_i$ have no upper bound. MTZ still eliminates every subtour: adding (3) along a cycle of properties gives $n \cdot |\text{cycle}| \le (n - 1) \cdot |\text{cycle}|$, which is impossible.
- The agency is excluded from (3), so the single tour must pass through node $0$.
