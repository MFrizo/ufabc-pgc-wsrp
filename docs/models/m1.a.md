# M1.a — M1 with Flexible Visits (MO-DOMDVRPTW-SD)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m1_a` in `src/models/model_1_a.py`
- **Role:** subcase of [M1](m1.md)
- **Instance settings:** M1's (`num_brokers = 3`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = NORMAL_SPEED`, `start_at_homes = True`, `lunch_break = (180, 360, 60)`, `max_day_length = 720`) with `fixed_ratio = 0.5`; default seed 8. `--fixed-ratio` in `main.py` overrides the share.

[M1](m1.md) where only some visits were booked by the visitors. The booked visits keep M1's soft window: they start at their scheduled time, up to 10 minutes early or late, and each minute late is penalized. Every other visit is flexible: it may start at any time of its window, which spans the whole working day, and is never late. Everything else is M1's: homes, open routes, lunch break, overtime, 12-hour days, and the brokers first in the objective, then travel time.

The share of booked visits is the instance's `fixed_ratio`. Comparing M1.a runs at different ratios, and with M1 (every visit booked), shows how much the strict schedule weighs on the solution and on the solver.

## Sets

All of [M1](m1.md)'s sets ($K$, $C$, $D$, $F$, $A$, $V$ and $\mathcal{A}$), with the properties split in two:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $C_h \subseteq C$ | `model.C_h` | `fixed_visits` | Booked visits, with a scheduled start |
| $C_w = C \setminus C_h$ | `model.C_w` | — | Flexible visits, with a window |

## Parameters

All of [M1](m1.md)'s parameters, except that the scheduled start exists only for the booked visits and the flexible visits get a window instead:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $h_i$ | `model.scheduled` | `earliest_start` | Scheduled start of booked visit $i \in C_h$ |
| $e_i$ | `model.earliest` | `earliest_start` | Earliest start of flexible visit $i \in C_w$ |
| $l_i$ | `model.latest` | `latest_start` | Latest start of flexible visit $i \in C_w$ |

The generator gives every flexible visit the whole day: $e_i = 0$ and $l_i = \text{horizon} - s_i$, so it ends by the end of the generator's day (600 minutes, 08:00 to 18:00, extended if the hidden schedule needs more).

$H$ and $W_{frota}$ are computed as in M1, with each kind of visit's own latest end, and with only the booked visits able to be late:

$$
\begin{aligned}
H &= \max\Big(\max_{i \in C_h} (h_i + \tau + s_i),\ \max_{i \in C_w} (l_i + s_i),\ \overline{\lambda} + 60,\ \max_{k \in K} E_k\Big) \\
M &= H + \max_{i \in V} s_i + \max_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij} \\
U &= (n + m) \max_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij} \\
W_{dist} &= 1/(U + 1) \quad \text{unless } \texttt{distance\_weight} \text{ is given} \\
W_{frota} &= (n + m) \max_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij}\, W_{time} + U\, W_{dist} + |C_h|\, \tau\, W_{atraso} + \sum_{k \in K} \max(0,\ H - L_k)\, W_{extra} + 1
\end{aligned}
$$

## Decision variables

Same as [M1](m1.md): $x_{ijk}$, $y_k$, $w_i \in [0, H]$, $\delta_i$ and $o_k$. The delay variable is still declared for every property, but it is fixed to $0$ for the flexible visits:

$$
\delta_i = 0 \qquad \forall i \in C_w
$$

## Objective function

Minimize the brokers who work first, then travel time, delay of the booked visits and overtime, with distance as a tie-break (`model.obj`):

$$
\min\ W_{frota} \sum_{k \in K} y_k
+ W_{time} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk}
+ W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk}
+ W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C_h} \delta_i
+ W_{extra} \sum_{k \in K} o_k
$$

## Constraints

Constraints (1) to (6), (8) and (9) of [M1](m1.md) are unchanged: single service, compatibility, home departure, end of the day, flow conservation, time flow, working day and lunch break. Constraint (7) is split by kind of visit.

**(7a) Soft time windows of the booked visits** (`early_start_constraint`, `soft_window_constraint`, `delay_tolerance_constraint`):

$$
w_i \ge h_i - \tau, \qquad w_i \le h_i + \delta_i, \qquad \delta_i \le \tau \qquad \forall i \in C_h
$$

**(7b) Hard windows of the flexible visits** (`flexible_window_constraint`):

$$
e_i \le w_i \le l_i \qquad \forall i \in C_w
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & W_{frota} \sum_{k \in K} y_k + W_{time} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk} + W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk} + W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C_h} \delta_i + W_{extra} \sum_{k \in K} o_k \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} && \forall i \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{j : (d_k, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{d_k j k} = y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, f_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i f_k k} = y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, h, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j : (h, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjk} = 0 && \forall k \in K,\ h \in C \cup \{a_k\} \\
& w_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - M\,(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& h_i - \tau \le w_i \le h_i + \delta_i,\quad \delta_i \le \tau && \forall i \in C_h \\
& e_i \le w_i \le l_i,\quad \delta_i = 0 && \forall i \in C_w \\
& w_{d_k} \ge E_k,\quad w_{f_k} \le L_k + o_k,\quad w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} \le J_{max} && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{(i, j) : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} (s_i + t^k_{ij})\, x_{ijk} \le J_{max}\, y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i \in C} s_i + \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij}\, x_{ijk} \le \sum_{k \in K} (J_{max} - s_{a_k})\, y_k \\
& \sum_{i : (i, a_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i a_k k} = y_k,\quad \underline{\lambda} \le w_{a_k} \le \overline{\lambda} && \forall k \in K \\
& x_{ijk} \in \{0, 1\} && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& y_k \in \{0, 1\},\ o_k \ge 0 && \forall k \in K \\
& 0 \le w_i \le H && \forall i \in V \\
& \delta_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in C
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- **Relation to M1:** with `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, $C_w$ is empty and M1.a is exactly M1. With `fixed_ratio = 0`, every visit is flexible and no visit can be late.
- **Flexible visits have no preferred time:** any start inside $[e_i, l_i]$ costs the same, so their start times are driven only by the route, the lunch break and the working day.
- The remarks of [M1](m1.md) on open routes, on the virtual lunch node (zero distance and time into and out of it), on overtime and on units all apply unchanged.
