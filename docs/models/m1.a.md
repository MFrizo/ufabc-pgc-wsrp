# M1.a — M1 with Tightened and Lifted MTZ Time-Flow Constraints (MO-DOMDVRPTW-SD)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m1_a` in `src/models/model_1_a.py`
- **Role:** tightened and lifted reformulation of [M1](m1.md) without a single global Big-M constant
- **Instance settings:** same as [M1](m1.md) (`num_brokers = 3`, `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = NORMAL_SPEED`, `start_at_homes = True`, `lunch_break = (180, 360, 60)`, `max_day_length = 720`; default seed 8). `--fixed-ratio` in `main.py` overrides the share of booked visits from `0.0` to `1.0`.

[M1](m1.md) deactivates the time-flow inequality $w_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j$ with a single global constant $M = H + \max s_i + \max t^k_{ij}$. When $x_{ijk}$ is fractional in the linear relaxation, that global $M$ leaves the time variables loosely coupled to the routing variables and forces the solver to generate mixed-integer rounding (MIR) and implied-bound cuts.

M1.a keeps the exact same feasible set and objective function as [M1](m1.md) for every `fixed_ratio`, and replaces the global Big-M time-flow formulation with three tightenings:

1. **Time-window preprocessing (domain reduction)** by iterative forward and backward network reachability, plus fixing time-infeasible arcs ($a_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} > b_j$) to zero;
2. **Arc-specific tightened $M^k_{ij} = \max(0,\ b_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - a_j)$**, aggregated across brokers on property-to-property pairs $(i, j) \in C \times C$;
3. **Desrochers and Laporte (1991) lifted time-window bounds** and bidirectional two-cycle elimination.

## Sets

All of [M1](m1.md)'s sets ($K$, $C$, $C_h$, $C_w$, $D$, $F$, $A$, $V$ and $\mathcal{A}$), plus the ordered and unordered property pairs:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $P_{\text{ord}} = \lbrace (i, j) \in C \times C : i \ne j \rbrace$ | `model.PAIRS` | Ordered pairs of distinct properties |
| $P_{<} = \lbrace (i, j) \in C \times C : i < j \rbrace$ | `model.UPPER_PAIRS` | Unordered pairs of distinct properties |

## Parameters and time-window preprocessing

All of [M1](m1.md)'s parameters, except that the global constant `model.big_m` is replaced by tightened node windows $[a_i, b_i]$ and arc-specific coefficients $M^k_{ij}$:

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $a_i, b_i$ | `model.window_start`, `model.window_end` | Tightened earliest and latest start time at node $i \in V$ |
| $M^k_{ij}$ | `model.arc_big_m` | Tightened time-flow coefficient of arc $(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}$ |

### 1. Pre-processing time windows (domain reduction)

Each node $i \in V$ starts with its initial time window $[a_i^{(0)}, b_i^{(0)}]$:

$$
[a_i^{(0)}, b_i^{(0)}] =
\begin{cases}
[\max(0,\ h_i - \tau),\ h_i + \tau] & i \in C_h \\
[e_i,\ l_i] & i \in C_w \\
[\underline{\lambda},\ \overline{\lambda}] & i = a_k \in A \\
[E_k,\ \max(E_k,\ \min(H, \overline{\lambda}))] & i = d_k \in D \\
[E_k,\ H] & i = f_k \in F
\end{cases}
$$

Let $\mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}$ be the set of compatible, unblocked arcs $(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}$ that satisfy $a_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} \le b_j$. Before building the constraints, $a_j$ and $b_i$ are tightened iteratively until convergence for all $i, j \in C$:

$$
a_j \leftarrow \max\Big(a_j,\ \min_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}} (a_i + s_i + t^k_{ij})\Big), \qquad
b_i \leftarrow \min\Big(b_i,\ \max_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} _ {\text{active}}} (b_j - s_i - t^k_{ij})\Big)
$$

Any arc $(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}$ whose earliest arrival exceeds the destination's latest start ($a_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} > b_j$) cannot be used in any feasible schedule and is fixed to zero:

$$
x_{ijk} = 0 \qquad \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \text{ with } a_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} > b_j
$$

### 2. Tightening the arc-specific parameter $M^k_{ij}$

Rewriting the time propagation $w_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - M^k_{ij}(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j$ as

$$
w_i - w_j + M^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} \le M^k_{ij} - s_i - t^k_{ij}
$$

shows that when $x_{ijk} = 0$, the largest value the left-hand side $w_i - w_j$ can take over $w_i \in [a_i, b_i]$ and $w_j \in [a_j, b_j]$ is $b_i - a_j$. Setting $M^k_{ij} - s_i - t^k_{ij} = b_i - a_j$ yields the smallest valid coefficient for each directed arc:

$$
M^k_{ij} = \max\Big(0,\ b_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} - a_j\Big) \qquad \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}
$$

When $b_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} \le a_j$, $M^k_{ij} = 0$ and the variable bounds $w_i \le b_i$ and $w_j \ge a_j$ already enforce $w_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} \le w_j$, so the inequality is omitted.

## Decision variables and objective function

Same as [M1](m1.md): $x_{ijk} \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$, $y_k \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace$, $w_i \in [a_i, b_i]$, $\delta_i \ge 0$ (with $\delta_i = 0$ for $i \in C_w$) and $o_k \ge 0$, with the same objective function (`model.obj`).

## Constraints

Constraints (1) to (5), (7a), (7b), (8) and (9) of [M1](m1.md) are unchanged. Constraint (6) is replaced by the tightened and lifted propagation and window-bound inequalities below.

**(6a) Broker-lifted time flow between properties** (`pair_time_flow_constraint`). Because at most one broker traverses $(i, j) \in P_{\text{ord}}$, summing $M^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk}$ over $k \in K$ into a single row replaces $|K|$ separate rows with one tighter inequality:

$$
w_i - w_j + \sum_{k \in K} M^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} \le b_i - a_j \qquad \forall (i, j) \in P_{\text{ord}}
$$

**(6b) Arc-specific time flow on depot, lunch and end arcs** (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i - w_j + M^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} \le b_i - a_j \qquad \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \text{ with } (i, j) \notin P_{\text{ord}}
$$

**(6c) Desrochers and Laporte (1991) lifted time-window bounds** (`lifted_early_constraint`, `lifted_late_constraint`). When broker $k$ enters node $i$ from $j$ ($x_{jik} = 1$), $w_i$ cannot start before $a_j + s_j + t^k_{ji}$; when broker $k$ leaves node $i$ for $j$ ($x_{ijk} = 1$), $w_i$ cannot start after $b_j - s_i - t^k_{ij}$:

$$
w_i \ge a_i + \sum_{(j, i, k) \in \mathcal{A}} \max\Big(0,\ a_j + s_j + t^k_{ji} - a_i\Big) x_{jik} \qquad \forall i \in C \cup A
$$

$$
w_i \le b_i - \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} \max\Big(0,\ b_i - (b_j - s_i - t^k_{ij})\Big) x_{ijk} \qquad \forall i \in C \cup A
$$

**(6d) Bidirectional two-cycle elimination** (`two_cycle_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{k \in K} (x_{ijk} + x_{jik}) \le 1 \qquad \forall (i, j) \in P_{<}
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & W_{frota} \sum_{k \in K} y_k + W_{time} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} + W_{dist} \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} c^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} + W_{atraso} \sum_{i \in C_h} \delta_i + W_{extra} \sum_{k \in K} o_k \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} && \forall i \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{j : (d_k, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{d_k j k} = y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, f_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i f_k k} = y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i : (i, h, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j : (h, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{hjk} = 0 && \forall k \in K,\ h \in C \cup \lbrace a_k \rbrace \\
& w_i - w_j + \sum_{k \in K} M^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} \le b_i - a_j && \forall (i, j) \in P_{\text{ord}} \\
& w_i - w_j + M^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} \le b_i - a_j && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A},\ (i, j) \notin P_{\text{ord}} \\
& \sum_{k \in K} (x_{ijk} + x_{jik}) \le 1 && \forall (i, j) \in P_{<} \\
& w_i \ge a_i + \sum_{(j, i, k) \in \mathcal{A}} \max(0,\ a_j + s_j + t^k_{ji} - a_i)\ x_{jik} && \forall i \in C \cup A \\
& w_i \le b_i - \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} \max(0,\ b_i - b_j + s_i + t^k_{ij})\ x_{ijk} && \forall i \in C \cup A \\
& h_i - \tau \le w_i \le h_i + \delta_i,\quad \delta_i \le \tau && \forall i \in C_h \\
& a_i \le w_i \le b_i,\quad \delta_i = 0 && \forall i \in C_w \\
& w_{d_k} \ge E_k,\quad w_{f_k} \le L_k + o_k,\quad w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} \le J_{max} && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{(i, j) : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} (s_i + t^k_{ij})\ x_{ijk} \le J_{max}\ y_k && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{i \in C} s_i + \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} t^k_{ij}\ x_{ijk} \le \sum_{k \in K} (J_{max} - s_{a_k})\ y_k \\
& \sum_{i : (i, a_k, k) \in \mathcal{A}} x_{i a_k k} = y_k,\quad \underline{\lambda} \le w_{a_k} \le \overline{\lambda} && \forall k \in K \\
& x_{ijk} = 0 && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A},\ a_i + s_i + t^k_{ij} > b_j \\
& x_{ijk} \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace && \forall (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A} \\
& y_k \in \lbrace 0, 1 \rbrace,\ o_k \ge 0 && \forall k \in K \\
& a_i \le w_i \le b_i && \forall i \in V \\
& \delta_i \ge 0 && \forall i \in C
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- **Exact equivalence on integer solutions:** none of the tightenings cuts off a feasible integer schedule of [M1](m1.md). Every seed and `fixed_ratio` has the exact same optimal objective value as M1.
- **Why the reverse-arc term $(b_i - a_j - s_j - t_{ji}) x_{jik}$ is not placed inside $(6a)$:** Desrochers and Laporte (1991, eq. 11) lifted $x_{ji}$ into the $w_i - w_j$ inequality with coefficient $b_i - a_j - s_j - t_{ji}$. As shown by Yuan, Cattaruzza, Ogier and Semet (2020), when $x_{jik} = 1$ that coefficient reduces $w_i - w_j \le b_i - a_j$ to $w_i \le w_j + s_j + t^k_{ji}$, which forbids waiting at node $i$ and cuts off optimal schedules whenever a broker arrives early and waits for a visit window or lunch window to open. In M1.a, reverse-arc coupling is enforced instead through the lifted window-bound inequalities $(6c)$ (Desrochers and Laporte, 1991, eqs. 8–9) and the two-cycle inequality $(6d)$, which remain valid under arbitrary waiting times.
