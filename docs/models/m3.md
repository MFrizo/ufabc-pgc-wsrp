# M3 — Site-Dependent Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (SDVRPTW)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m3` in `src/models/model_3.py`
- **Case:** 4
- **Instance settings:** `num_brokers = 2`, `fixed_ratio = 1.0`; 40% of the visits already have a broker (`assigned_ratio` default)

[M2](m2.md) where some visits were booked with a specific broker, and only that broker may do them.

## Sets

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $V = \{0, 1, \dots, n\}$ | `model.V` | All nodes; node $0$ is the agency |
| $C = V \setminus \{0\}$ | `model.C` | Properties to visit |
| $K = \{1, \dots, m\}$ | `model.K` | Brokers |

## Parameters

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c_{ij}$ | `model.distance` | `distance_matrix` | Distance from $i$ to $j$ |
| $T$ | `model.travel_time` | `travel_time` | Travel time of every trip |
| $s_i$ | `model.service` | `service_time` | Duration of the visit at $i$: $s_0 = 0$, $s_i = S$ for $i \in C$ |
| $e_i$, $l_i$ | `model.earliest`, `model.latest` | `earliest_start`, `latest_start` | Time window of node $i$ |
| $P_{ik}$ | `model.eligibility` | `assigned_broker` | $1$ if broker $k$ may visit property $i$ |
| $M$ | `model.big_m` | `big_m` (optional) | Big-M constant, computed as in [M1](m1.md) |

With $a_i$ the broker already assigned to property $i$ ($a_i = 0$ if none), $P_{ik} = 1$ if $a_i \in \{0, k\}$ and $P_{ik} = 0$ otherwise.

## Decision variables

| Symbol | Pyomo | Domain | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x_{ijk}$ | `model.x` | $\{0, 1\}$, $i, j \in V$, $k \in K$ | $1$ if broker $k$ travels directly from $i$ to $j$ |
| $w_i$ | `model.w` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $i \in V$ | Start of the visit at $i$; $w_0$ is the departure from the agency |

## Objective function

Minimize the total distance travelled by all brokers (`model.obj`):

$$
\min \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} c_{ij}\, x_{ijk}
$$

## Constraints

**(1) Single service** (`single_service_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} = 1 \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(2) Compatibility** — a broker only leaves a property he may visit (`compatibility_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} \qquad \forall i \in C,\ k \in K
$$

**(3) Flow conservation** (`flow_conservation_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne h} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne h} x_{hjk} = 0 \qquad \forall h \in C,\ k \in K
$$

**(4) Agency departure** (`base_departure_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{0jk} \le 1 \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(5) Time flow** (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i + s_i + T - M\,(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j \qquad \forall i \in V,\ j \in C,\ k \in K,\ i \ne j
$$

**(6) Time windows** (`time_window_constraint`):

$$
e_i \le w_i \le l_i \qquad \forall i \in V
$$

**(7) No self-loops** (`no_self_loop_constraint`):

$$
x_{iik} = 0 \qquad \forall i \in V,\ k \in K
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} c_{ij}\, x_{ijk} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} && \forall i \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne h} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne h} x_{hjk} = 0 && \forall h \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{j \in C} x_{0jk} \le 1 && \forall k \in K \\
& w_i + s_i + T - M\,(1 - x_{ijk}) \le w_j && \forall i \in V,\ j \in C,\ k \in K,\ i \ne j \\
& e_i \le w_i \le l_i && \forall i \in V \\
& x_{iik} = 0 && \forall i \in V,\ k \in K \\
& x_{ijk} \in \{0, 1\},\ w_i \ge 0 && \forall i, j \in V,\ k \in K
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- Each assigned visit goes to the broker who does it in the hidden reference schedule, so the assignments never make an instance infeasible.
- The remarks of [M2](m2.md) on $w_0$ and on the return to the agency still apply.
