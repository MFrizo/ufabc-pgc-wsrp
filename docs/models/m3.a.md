# M3.a — M3 with Tightened and Lifted MTZ and Shift-Overlap Bounds

- **Builder:** `build_model_m3_a` in `src/models/model_3_a.py`
- **Front:** `pareto_front` in `src/models/model_3_a.py`
- **Role:** tightened and lifted reformulation of [M3](m3.md) built on [M1.a](m1.a.md) without any global Big-M constant
- **Instance settings:** same as [M3](m3.md) ([M1](m1.md)'s settings plus `days_off = 1`; default seed 8). `--fixed-ratio` in `main.py` overrides the share of booked visits from `0.0` to `1.0`.

[M3](m3.md) builds on [M1](m1.md) and uses the global constant $M$ in six shift-overlap and outside-shift inequalities (`reach_shift_constraint`, `not_reach_shift_constraint`, `start_before_shift_end_constraint`, `start_after_shift_end_constraint`, `overlap_in_route_constraint`, and `outside_constraint`).

M3.a builds on [M1.a](m1.a.md) instead of [M1](m1.md), inheriting [M1.a](m1.a.md)'s preprocessed node windows $[a_i, b_i]$, arc-specific $M^k_{ij}$, broker-lifted time flow, and Desrochers–Laporte window bounds, and removes the global $M$ from all shift-overlap and lunch-pair constraints:

1. **Exact shift/broker-specific activation constants** derived from the tightened origin and destination windows $[a_{d_k}, b_{d_k}]$ and $[a_{f_k}, b_{f_k}]$ and each shift $[E_{ks}, L_{ks}]$;
2. **Big-M-free overlap and outside-shift bounds** obtained by bounding $o_{ks} \le (L_{ks} - E_{ks}) y_k$ and enforcing $w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} \ge \sum_{(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} (s_i + t^k_{ij}) x_{ijk}$;
3. **Time-infeasible lunch-pair pruning** fixing $z_{ijk} = 0$ whenever $x_{i, a_k, k}$ or $x_{a_k, j, k}$ is fixed to zero or $a_i + s_i + s_{a_k} + t^k_{ij} > b_j$.

## Sets and parameters

All of [M1.a](m1.a.md)'s and [M3](m3.md)'s sets and parameters. For each working broker $k \in K_{\text{on}}$ and each of his shifts $s$ with window $[E_{ks}, L_{ks}]$ of length $\Delta_{ks} = L_{ks} - E_{ks}$, the tightest valid indicator bounds from the preprocessed windows $[a_{d_k}, b_{d_k}]$ and $[a_{f_k}, b_{f_k}]$ are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
M^{\text{reach+}}_{ks} &= \max\Big(0,\ b_{f_k} - E_{ks}\Big), &
M^{\text{reach-}}_{ks} &= \max\Big(0,\ E_{ks} - a_{f_k}\Big), \\
M^{\text{start+}}_{ks} &= \max\Big(0,\ b_{d_k} - L_{ks}\Big), &
M^{\text{start-}}_{ks} &= \max\Big(0,\ L_{ks} - a_{d_k}\Big)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Decision variables and objectives

Same as [M3](m3.md) on top of [M1.a](m1.a.md), with $o_{ks} \in [0, \Delta_{ks}]$ (`model.overlap`).

## Constraints

Constraints of [M1.a](m1.a.md) and constraint (10) ($\sum_{k \in K} y_k \le m$) of [M3](m3.md) are unchanged. Constraints (11) and (12) of [M3](m3.md) are replaced by the tightened formulations below.

**(11a) Shift-overlap indicator constraints with tight constants** (`reach_shift_constraint`, `not_reach_shift_constraint`, `start_before_shift_end_constraint`, `start_after_shift_end_constraint`):

$$
\begin{aligned}
w_{f_k} - E_{ks} &\le M^{\text{reach+}}_{ks}\ r_{ks}, &
E_{ks} - w_{f_k} &\le M^{\text{reach-}}_{ks}\ (1 - r_{ks}), \\
L_{ks} - w_{d_k} &\le M^{\text{start-}}_{ks}\ q_{ks}, &
w_{d_k} - L_{ks} &\le M^{\text{start+}}_{ks}\ (1 - q_{ks})
\end{aligned}
$$

**(11b) Tight overlap upper bounds without global Big-M** (`overlap_active_constraint`, `overlap_length_constraint`, `overlap_after_start_constraint`, `overlap_before_end_constraint`, `overlap_in_route_constraint`):

$$
\begin{aligned}
o_{ks} &\le \Delta_{ks}\ y_k, \qquad o_{ks} \le \Delta_{ks}\ r_{ks}, \qquad o_{ks} \le \Delta_{ks}\ q_{ks}, \\
o_{ks} &\le L_{ks} - w_{d_k} + M^{\text{start+}}_{ks}\ (1 - q_{ks}), \\
o_{ks} &\le w_{f_k} - E_{ks} + M^{\text{reach-}}_{ks}\ (1 - r_{ks}), \\
o_{ks} &\le w_{f_k} - w_{d_k}
\end{aligned}
$$

**(11c) Big-M-free outside-shift duration and linear route-span lower bound** (`outside_constraint`, `route_span_lower_constraint`):

$$
u_k \ge w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} - \sum_{s} o_{ks} \qquad \forall k \in K_{\text{on}}
$$

$$
w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} \ge \sum_{(i, j) : (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{A}} (s_i + t^k_{ij})\ x_{ijk} \qquad \forall k \in K_{\text{on}}
$$

Because $w_{d_k}, w_{f_k} \in [E_k, H]$ can always be chosen equal when $y_k = 0$ (and $o_{ks} = 0$), the inequality $u_k \ge w_{f_k} - w_{d_k} - \sum_s o_{ks}$ requires no $-M(1 - y_k)$ term at all.

**(12) Pruned lunch-pair linking** (`lunch_cover_constraint`, `lunch_predecessor_constraint`, `lunch_successor_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i, j} z_{ijk} = y_k, \qquad
\sum_{j} z_{ijk} = x_{i, a_k, k}, \qquad
\sum_{i} z_{ijk} = x_{a_k, j, k}
$$

with $z_{ijk} = 0$ whenever $x_{i, a_k, k}$ or $x_{a_k, j, k}$ is fixed to zero or $a_i + s_i + s_{a_k} + t^k_{ij} > b_j$.

## Remarks

- **Exact equivalence on the Pareto front:** for every fleet cap $m \in \lbrace 1, \dots, |K| \rbrace$, every seed, and every `fixed_ratio` in $[0, 1]$, `build_model_m3_a` and `pareto_front` in `src/models/model_3_a.py` produce the exact same optimal `broker_cost` and Pareto front as [M3](m3.md).
