# M5 — Time-Dependent Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (TDVRPTW)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m5` in `src/models/model_5.py`
- **Case:** 6
- **Instance settings:** `num_brokers = 2`, `fixed_ratio = 1.0`, `service_time_variation = 30`, `speed_profile = RUSH_HOURS`

[M4](m4.md) where a trip's travel time depends on the period of the day it leaves in, and the brokers' total time out of the agency is minimized instead of the distance. With `RUSH_HOURS`, the day has three periods: 08:00–10:00 and from 17:00 at 1 unit/min, 10:00–17:00 at 2 units/min.

## Sets

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $V = \{0, 1, \dots, n\}$ | `model.V` | All nodes; node $0$ is the agency |
| $C = V \setminus \{0\}$ | `model.C` | Properties to visit |
| $K = \{1, \dots, m\}$ | `model.K` | Brokers |
| $\mathcal{P} = \{0, 1, \dots, q\}$ | `model.P` | Periods of the day, each with its own travel times |

## Parameters

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c_{ij}$ | `model.distance` | `distance_matrix` | Distance from $i$ to $j$ (declared, not used by the objective) |
| $t^p_{ij}$ | `model.travel_time` | `travel_times` | Travel time from $i$ to $j$ of a trip leaving in period $p$ |
| $b_p$ | `model.period_start` | `period_starts` | Start of period $p$ |
| $\bar b_p$ | `model.period_end` | — | End of period $p$ |
| $s_i$ | `model.service` | `service_times` | Duration of the visit at $i$; $s_0 = 0$ |
| $e_i$, $l_i$ | `model.earliest`, `model.latest` | `earliest_start`, `latest_start` | Time window of node $i$ |
| $P_{ik}$ | `model.eligibility` | `assigned_broker` | $1$ if broker $k$ may visit property $i$ (see [M3](m3.md)) |
| $M$ | `model.big_m` | `big_m` (optional) | Big-M constant |

Each period ends where the next one starts, $\bar b_p = b_{p+1}$. The last one ends at the latest possible departure, $\bar b_q = \max\big(b_q,\ \max_{i \in V} (l_i + s_i)\big)$.

Unless given, $M$ is computed by M5's `compute_big_m`:

$$
M = \max\Big(0,\ \max_{p \in \mathcal{P}}\ \max_{i, j \in V,\ i \ne j} \big(l_i + s_i + t^p_{ij} - e_j\big)\Big)
$$

## Decision variables

| Symbol | Pyomo | Domain | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x_{ijk}$ | `model.x` | $\{0, 1\}$, $i, j \in V$, $k \in K$ | $1$ if broker $k$ travels directly from $i$ to $j$ |
| $w_i$ | `model.w` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $i \in C$ | Start of the visit at property $i$ |
| $w^{dep}_k$ | `model.w_departure` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $k \in K$ | Time broker $k$ leaves the agency |
| $w^{ret}_k$ | `model.w_return` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $k \in K$ | Time broker $k$ gets back to the agency |
| $z_{ip}$ | `model.z` | $\{0, 1\}$, $i \in C$, $p \in \mathcal{P}$ | $1$ if the broker leaves property $i$ (at $w_i + s_i$) in period $p$ |
| $z^0_{kp}$ | `model.z_base` | $\{0, 1\}$, $k \in K$, $p \in \mathcal{P}$ | $1$ if broker $k$ leaves the agency in period $p$ |

## Objective function

Minimize the brokers' total time out of the agency (`model.obj`):

$$
\min \sum_{k \in K} \big(w^{ret}_k - w^{dep}_k\big)
$$

## Constraints

**(1) Single service** (`single_service_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} = 1 \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

**(2) Compatibility** (`compatibility_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} \qquad \forall i \in C,\ k \in K
$$

**(3) Flow conservation** (`flow_conservation_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne h} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne h} x_{hjk} = 0 \qquad \forall h \in C,\ k \in K
$$

**(4) Agency departure** (`base_departure_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{0jk} \le 1 \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(5) Departure periods** — every departure falls in exactly one period, inside its bounds; a departure exactly on a boundary may take either period.

From each property (`property_period_constraint`, `property_period_start_constraint`, `property_period_end_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} z_{ip} = 1, \qquad
\sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} b_p\, z_{ip} \le w_i + s_i \le \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \bar b_p\, z_{ip} \qquad \forall i \in C
$$

From the agency (`base_period_constraint`, `base_period_start_constraint`, `base_period_end_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} z^0_{kp} = 1, \qquad
\sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} b_p\, z^0_{kp} \le w^{dep}_k \le \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \bar b_p\, z^0_{kp} \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(6) Time flow** — with the travel time of the period the broker leaves in. The term $M\,(2 - x - z)$ switches the constraint off unless the arc is used and the period is the chosen one.

Between properties (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i + s_i + t^p_{ij} - M\,(2 - x_{ijk} - z_{ip}) \le w_j \qquad \forall i, j \in C,\ i \ne j,\ k \in K,\ p \in \mathcal{P}
$$

From the agency (`base_time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w^{dep}_k + t^p_{0j} - M\,(2 - x_{0jk} - z^0_{kp}) \le w_j \qquad \forall j \in C,\ k \in K,\ p \in \mathcal{P}
$$

Back to the agency (`return_time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i + s_i + t^p_{i0} - M\,(2 - x_{i0k} - z_{ip}) \le w^{ret}_k \qquad \forall i \in C,\ k \in K,\ p \in \mathcal{P}
$$

A broker who stays at the agency spends no time out of it (`return_after_departure_constraint`):

$$
w^{dep}_k \le w^{ret}_k \qquad \forall k \in K
$$

**(7) Time windows** — every visit starts inside its window and every broker leaves the agency inside the agency's window (`time_window_constraint`, `base_window_constraint`):

$$
e_i \le w_i \le l_i \quad \forall i \in C, \qquad e_0 \le w^{dep}_k \le l_0 \quad \forall k \in K
$$

**(8) No self-loops** (`no_self_loop_constraint`):

$$
x_{iik} = 0 \qquad \forall i \in V,\ k \in K
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \big(w^{ret}_k - w^{dep}_k\big) \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} = 1 && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ijk} \le P_{ik} && \forall i \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne h} x_{ihk} - \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne h} x_{hjk} = 0 && \forall h \in C,\ k \in K \\
& \sum_{j \in C} x_{0jk} \le 1 && \forall k \in K \\
& \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} z_{ip} = 1,\quad \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} b_p\, z_{ip} \le w_i + s_i \le \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \bar b_p\, z_{ip} && \forall i \in C \\
& \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} z^0_{kp} = 1,\quad \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} b_p\, z^0_{kp} \le w^{dep}_k \le \sum_{p \in \mathcal{P}} \bar b_p\, z^0_{kp} && \forall k \in K \\
& w_i + s_i + t^p_{ij} - M\,(2 - x_{ijk} - z_{ip}) \le w_j && \forall i, j \in C,\ i \ne j,\ k \in K,\ p \in \mathcal{P} \\
& w^{dep}_k + t^p_{0j} - M\,(2 - x_{0jk} - z^0_{kp}) \le w_j && \forall j \in C,\ k \in K,\ p \in \mathcal{P} \\
& w_i + s_i + t^p_{i0} - M\,(2 - x_{i0k} - z_{ip}) \le w^{ret}_k && \forall i \in C,\ k \in K,\ p \in \mathcal{P} \\
& w^{dep}_k \le w^{ret}_k && \forall k \in K \\
& e_i \le w_i \le l_i && \forall i \in C \\
& e_0 \le w^{dep}_k \le l_0 && \forall k \in K \\
& x_{iik} = 0 && \forall i \in V,\ k \in K \\
& x_{ijk},\ z_{ip},\ z^0_{kp} \in \{0, 1\};\quad w_i,\ w^{dep}_k,\ w^{ret}_k \ge 0
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- Unlike M1–M4, the agency has a departure and a return time per broker, so the time out of the agency can be measured. The return still has no upper limit; only the objective keeps it early.
- The travel time is a step function of the departure time: it is the one of the period the trip **leaves** in, even if the trip ends in another period. This does not enforce the FIFO property (leaving later never means arriving earlier): a broker may wait for a faster period and arrive sooner.
- The generator builds the hidden reference schedule with the slowest period on every trip, so the instance stays feasible whichever period each trip leaves in.
