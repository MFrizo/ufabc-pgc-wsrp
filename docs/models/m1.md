# M1 — Traveling Salesman Problem with Time Windows (TSPTW)

- **Builder:** `build_model_m1` in `src/models/model_1.py`
- **Case:** 2
- **Instance settings:** generator defaults ($T = 30$, $S = 60$, `fixed_ratio = 0.2`)

M0 with time: every visit lasts $S$ minutes, every trip takes $T$ minutes, and every visit starts inside its time window. The start-time variables $w_i$ with Big-M time-flow constraints replace MTZ as the subtour elimination.

## Sets

| Symbol | Pyomo | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $V = \{0, 1, \dots, n\}$ | `model.V` | All nodes; node $0$ is the agency |
| $C = V \setminus \{0\}$ | `model.C` | Properties to visit |

## Parameters

| Symbol | Pyomo | Data key | Definition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c_{ij}$ | `model.distance` | `distance_matrix` | Distance from $i$ to $j$ |
| $T$ | `model.travel_time` | `travel_time` | Travel time of every trip |
| $s_i$ | `model.service` | `service_time` | Duration of the visit at $i$: $s_0 = 0$, $s_i = S$ for $i \in C$ |
| $e_i$ | `model.earliest` | `earliest_start` | Earliest start at node $i$ |
| $l_i$ | `model.latest` | `latest_start` | Latest start at node $i$ |
| $M$ | `model.big_m` | `big_m` (optional) | Big-M constant |

The agency's window is the whole day: $e_0 = 0$ and $l_0$ is the day's horizon. Unless given, $M$ is computed by `compute_big_m`:

$$
M = \max\Big(0,\ \max_{i \in V,\ j \in C,\ i \ne j} \big(l_i + s_i + T - e_j\big)\Big)
$$

## Decision variables

| Symbol | Pyomo | Domain | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $x_{ij}$ | `model.x` | $\{0, 1\}$, $i, j \in V$ | $1$ if the broker travels directly from $i$ to $j$ |
| $w_i$ | `model.w` | $\mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$, $i \in V$ | Start of the visit at $i$; $w_0$ is the departure from the agency |

## Objective function

Minimize the total distance travelled (`model.obj`):

$$
\min \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} c_{ij}\, x_{ij}
$$

## Constraints

**(1) Out-degree** (`out_degree_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ij} = 1 \qquad \forall i \in V
$$

**(2) In-degree** (`in_degree_constraint`):

$$
\sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne j} x_{ij} = 1 \qquad \forall j \in V
$$

**(3) Time flow** — a visit starts only after the previous one ends and the broker travels to it (`time_flow_constraint`):

$$
w_i + s_i + T - M\,(1 - x_{ij}) \le w_j \qquad \forall i \in V,\ j \in C,\ i \ne j
$$

**(4) Time windows** (`time_window_constraint`):

$$
e_i \le w_i \le l_i \qquad \forall i \in V
$$

**(5) No self-loops** (`no_self_loop_constraint`):

$$
x_{ii} = 0 \qquad \forall i \in V
$$

## Complete formulation

$$
\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} c_{ij}\, x_{ij} \\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{j \in V,\, j \ne i} x_{ij} = 1 && \forall i \in V \\
& \sum_{i \in V,\, i \ne j} x_{ij} = 1 && \forall j \in V \\
& w_i + s_i + T - M\,(1 - x_{ij}) \le w_j && \forall i \in V,\ j \in C,\ i \ne j \\
& e_i \le w_i \le l_i && \forall i \in V \\
& x_{ii} = 0 && \forall i \in V \\
& x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\},\ w_i \ge 0 && \forall i, j \in V
\end{aligned}
$$

## Remarks

- (3) is not written for arcs into the agency ($j = 0$), so the return trip has no time limit; only the departure $w_0$ is bound by the agency's window.
- Booked visits have $e_i = l_i$, so their start is fixed. Flexible visits may start at any time that lets them end within the day: $e_i = 0$ and $l_i = \text{horizon} - s_i$.
